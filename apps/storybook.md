## What is Storybook?

[Storybook](https://dobiapps.com/storybook) is a free reading and English learning app from
[Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com). It holds a library of more than 1,000,000 titles:
short stories, animal adventures, self-help, children's books, comics and
audiobooks, plus interactive games and memes. You can read, listen and track
your progress in more than 100 languages, and it works offline.

## Features

- **1,000,000+ free English books, stories and comics** in many genres
- **Audiobooks** that bring the stories to life
- **Games, memes and comics** that make practice fun
- **Reading stats**: daily reading time, books finished and how your vocabulary grows
- **100+ languages** with full dictionary support and translations
- **Achievements and levels** to keep you motivated as you read more
- **Offline reading and listening**, anywhere
- **Completely free**: no subscriptions and no hidden fees

## Why stories work for learning English

Reading lots of stories at the right level (extensive reading) means you keep
meeting the same words in real context. To learn more:
