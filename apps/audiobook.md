## What is Audiobook?

[Audiobook](https://dobiapps.com/audiobook) is an English learning app from
[Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com). It has thousands of English audiobooks and
ebooks, sorted by CEFR level from A1 to C2. You listen to the narration while
the synced transcript highlights each line, so listening and reading practice
happen together.

## Features

### Practice English listening with audiobooks
English stories and audiobooks read in natural voices, across many genres and levels.

### English audiobooks by level, A1 to C2
Find books at your level (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 or C2) and move up step by step.
Not sure where to start? Read [How to Find Your CEFR English Level](https://dobiapps.com/blog/find-your-cefr-english-level).

### Read the ebook while you listen
The transcript scrolls and highlights along with the narration. For the research on why this works, see
[Does Reading While Listening to Audiobooks Improve English?](https://dobiapps.com/blog/reading-while-listening-english-fluency).

### Learn vocabulary as you read
Tap any word to see its meaning and IPA pronunciation and to hear it spoken. Save new words to your own vocabulary list.

### Instant translation
Translate a word, sentence or paragraph into your interface language with one tap.

### Track your progress
See your listening history, study time and how many audiobooks you have finished. A level system tracks the minutes you spend listening.

### Learn offline
Download audiobooks and transcripts and study anywhere, even without an internet connection.

### 100+ interface languages
English, Tiếng Việt, 中文, 한국어, 日本語, Español, Français, Deutsch, العربية, हिन्दी, ภาษาไทย, Bahasa Indonesia and many more.

## Who it is for

- Learners who want to practice English listening every day
- Readers who want to improve their English reading skills
- Anyone who wants to learn vocabulary in context and practice pronunciation
- Learners who want graded audiobooks matched to their CEFR level
- People who need to study offline, anytime and anywhere
