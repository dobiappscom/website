# A 30-Day English Listening and Reading Plan

*By [Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com) · [← All guides](../README.md#guides-in-this-repository)*

Thirty days won't make you fluent, but it is enough time to build a habit.
Research on habit formation suggests a new routine takes around 66 days on
average to feel automatic
([How to Build an English Learning Habit That Lasts](https://dobiapps.com/blog/building-daily-english-learning-habit)),
so treat this plan as the first half of that journey.

**Daily time:** 20–30 minutes. **Tools:** [Audiobook](https://dobiapps.com/audiobook)
for listening and [Storybook](https://dobiapps.com/storybook) for reading.

## Week 1: Find your level and set the routine

- Day 1: Find your CEFR level ([guide](https://dobiapps.com/blog/find-your-cefr-english-level)).
- Days 2–7: Listen to one short audiobook chapter a day at your level, reading the transcript as you listen.
- Pick a fixed time, such as after breakfast or on your commute, and stick to it.

## Week 2: Read more

- Read one easy story a day in Storybook. It should feel almost effortless.
  ([Why easy reading works](https://dobiapps.com/blog/comprehensible-input-english-reading-level))
- Save up to 5 new words a day, no more.
- Review the words you saved in week 1.

## Week 3: Repeat and shadow

- Reread or relisten to a favorite story from week 1 or 2
  ([Does Rereading a Book Build English Fluency?](https://dobiapps.com/blog/repeated-reading-english-fluency)).
- Shadow one paragraph a day: play it, pause, and repeat it aloud
  ([Shadowing guide](https://dobiapps.com/blog/shadowing-audiobooks-english-pronunciation)).

## Week 4: Level up

- Try one book a level higher. If you understand less than 98% of the words, go back down.
- Mix formats: a comic or graphic novel can be a nice break
  ([Comics and graphic novels for learning English](https://dobiapps.com/blog/learning-english-through-comics-graphic-novels)).
- Day 30: Compare your listening and reading stats with day 1.

## If you fall behind

Missing a day is normal. Motivation drops when practice feels like a chore
you didn't choose, so let yourself pick the books you enjoy. See
[Why English Learners Lose Motivation and Quit](https://dobiapps.com/blog/why-english-learners-lose-motivation-and-quit).
