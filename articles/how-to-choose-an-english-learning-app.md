# How to Choose an English Learning App for Your Level

*By [Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com) · [← All guides](../README.md#guides-in-this-repository)*

No single app teaches every English skill well. The best choice depends on
what you need most right now. Use the questions below to decide.

## 1. Which skill do you want to improve?

| Goal | Look for | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | Leveled audiobooks with a synced transcript | [Audiobook](https://dobiapps.com/audiobook) |
| Reading and vocabulary in context | A large library of graded stories | [Storybook](https://dobiapps.com/storybook) |
| A daily habit | Short lessons, streaks and reminders | Duolingo |
| Pronunciation | Speech feedback | ELSA Speak |
| Conversation | Native speaker exchange | HelloTalk |
| Long-term word memory | Spaced repetition flashcards | Anki |

A full comparison is in
[Top 5 Apps for Learning English in 2026](https://dobiapps.com/blog/top-5-apps-for-learning-english).

## 2. Does it match your level?

An app is only useful if its content is at your level. Look for CEFR levels
(A1–C2) rather than vague labels like "beginner". To find your level, see
[How to Find Your CEFR English Level](https://dobiapps.com/blog/find-your-cefr-english-level).

## 3. Audio, text, or both?

Research suggests the best format depends on your goal: text plus audio for
listening, text plus pictures for reading. See
[Audiobook or Ebook App: Which Fits Your English Goal?](https://dobiapps.com/blog/audiobook-or-ebook-app-for-learning-english).

## 4. Are games enough?

Games help in the short term, but most of their advantage fades within weeks
unless you keep reviewing. See
[Do English Learning Games Actually Improve Vocabulary?](https://dobiapps.com/blog/do-english-learning-games-improve-vocabulary).

## 5. Practical checks

- Does it work offline?
- Does it support your native language for translations?
- Is it free, or what does the subscription include?
- Does it show your progress?

Both Dobi Apps, [Audiobook](../apps/audiobook.md) and [Storybook](../apps/storybook.md),
work offline and support more than 100 languages.
