# Reading English Stories with Kids: A Bedtime Routine

*By [Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com) · [← All guides](../README.md#guides-in-this-repository)*

Bedtime is one of the easiest times to add English to a child's day. It is
calm and it happens every night, and children like hearing the same stories
again and again. Repetition is exactly what language learning needs.

## Why it works

Children who are read to every day hear far more words by the time they start
school. The research, and how to use it at bedtime, is covered in
[How to Build a Bedtime Story Routine That Teaches English](https://dobiapps.com/blog/bedtime-story-routine-english-kids).

## A simple 15-minute routine

1. **Pick together (2 min).** Let your child choose from two or three short stories.
   Having a choice keeps them interested.
2. **Listen first (5 min).** Play the audiobook version so your child hears natural
   pronunciation, and follow the pictures together.
3. **Talk about it (5 min).** Ask open questions: *"What happened?" "What do you think comes next?"*
   This is called dialogic reading.
4. **Repeat a favorite (3 min).** Reread a page they love. Rereading builds fluency
   ([evidence](https://dobiapps.com/blog/repeated-reading-english-fluency)).

## Tips

- Keep the stories short and a little below your child's level.
- Picture books and comics help young learners understand
  ([comics and graphic novels](https://dobiapps.com/blog/learning-english-through-comics-graphic-novels)).
- Keep the same routine every night; the regularity matters more than the length.
- Download stories in advance so you can read offline.
