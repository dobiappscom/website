# Dobi Apps — English Learning Apps: Audiobooks, Stories & Ebooks by Level

**Website:** [dobiapps.com](https://dobiapps.com) · **Blog:** [dobiapps.com/blog](https://dobiapps.com/blog) · **Audiobook site:** [audiobook.dobiapps.com](https://audiobook.dobiapps.com)

[Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com) makes mobile apps that help people learn English through
reading and listening. You read graded stories, listen to audiobooks at your
CEFR level (A1 to C2), and pick up vocabulary from the stories themselves.
The apps are on the App Store and Google Play and work in more than 100
interface languages.

This repository is the public information hub for Dobi Apps. It covers the
apps, the blog and every official link in one place.

### Audiobook: English audiobooks by CEFR level

- Thousands of English audiobooks and ebooks, sorted by level from A1 to C2
- The ebook scrolls and highlights in sync with the narration
- Tap any word to see its meaning and IPA and hear it spoken, then save it to your word list
- Translate a word, sentence or paragraph with one tap
- Download books and transcripts to listen offline

➡️ [Learn more about Audiobook](https://dobiapps.com/audiobook) · [Audiobook blog](https://dobiapps.com/audiobook/blog)

### Storybook: learn English with stories

- A free library of more than 1,000,000 books, stories, comics and audiobooks
- Interactive games, memes and fun content alongside the reading
- Tracks reading time, books finished and how your vocabulary grows
- Dictionary and translation support in more than 100 languages
- Offline reading and listening. Completely free, with no subscription

➡️ [Learn more about Storybook](https://dobiapps.com/storybook) · [Storybook blog](https://dobiapps.com/storybook/blog)

## Về Dobi Apps (Tiếng Việt)

[Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com/vi) phát triển các ứng dụng giúp bạn học tiếng Anh qua
sách nói (audiobook), truyện và ebook theo trình độ CEFR từ A1 đến C2:

- **[Audiobook – Sách nói tiếng Anh theo trình độ](https://dobiapps.com/vi/audiobook)**: vừa nghe
  audiobook vừa đọc ebook có transcript đồng bộ, tra từ kèm phiên âm IPA, dịch nhanh và nghe offline.
- **[Storybook – Học tiếng Anh qua truyện](https://dobiapps.com/vi/storybook)**: hơn 1.000.000 sách,
  truyện, truyện tranh và audiobook miễn phí, hỗ trợ hơn 100 ngôn ngữ.

Đọc thêm các bài hướng dẫn học tiếng Anh tại [blog Dobi Apps](https://dobiapps.com/blog) (tiếng Anh).
