- **[How to Learn English Effectively Through Stories](learn-english-through-stories.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/learn-english-through-stories)
  Stories teach English through extensive reading, where you meet words again and again in real context. Covers how to pick the right level, plus 5 apps to try.
- **[Comprehensible Input: Find Your Right English Level](comprehensible-input-english-reading-level.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/comprehensible-input-english-reading-level)
  Comprehensible input is text you can understand almost completely. A landmark 2000 study found that 98% word coverage is the threshold. Includes how to test your own level.
- **[Does Rereading a Book Build English Fluency?](repeated-reading-english-fluency.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/repeated-reading-english-fluency)
  A National Reading Panel review found that repeated reading raises fluency, and a 2025 study found that narrow reading raised L2 vocabulary scores.

## Listening & audiobooks

- **[Does Reading While Listening to Audiobooks Improve English?](reading-while-listening-english-fluency.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/reading-while-listening-english-fluency)
  A 26-week EFL study found that reading while listening raised listening scores and produced 4x the vocabulary gain of a control group.
- **[Does Audiobook Playback Speed Affect English Comprehension?](audiobook-listening-speed-english-comprehension.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/audiobook-listening-speed-english-comprehension)
  Studies from 1997 and 2024 found that slowing audio down helps, and that the best speed depends on proficiency level.
- **[Does Shadowing Audiobooks Improve English Pronunciation?](shadowing-audiobooks-english-pronunciation.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/shadowing-audiobooks-english-pronunciation)
  Shadowing means speaking along with the audio in real time. A 2025 study found it raised EFL listening scores by 32% in 8 weeks.
- **[Audiobook or Ebook App: Which Fits Your English Goal?](audiobook-or-ebook-app-for-learning-english.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/audiobook-or-ebook-app-for-learning-english)
  A 186-learner EFL study found text plus audio gave the largest listening gains and text plus pictures the largest reading gains.
- **[Do Subtitles Help You Learn English Vocabulary?](subtitles-captioned-video-english-vocabulary.md)** · [dobiapps.com ↗](https://dobiapps.com/blog/subtitles-captioned-video-english-vocabulary)
  A 332-learner study compared bilingual subtitles with no subtitles and looked at who they help and who they don't.
